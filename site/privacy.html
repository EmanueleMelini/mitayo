In breve

Mitayo non ha account né server suoi. Accedi con il tuo account MyAnimeList sulla pagina di MyAnimeList, e tutto quello che l'app usa (la tua lista e gli anime ignorati) resta sul telefono. L'app comunica con MyAnimeList, con GitHub per gli aggiornamenti e con Google Firebase per le notifiche delle novità. Le statistiche d'uso e i report dei crash partono spenti e si attivano solo se acconsenti; non contengono mai la tua lista, i titoli o il tuo nome MyAnimeList. Niente pubblicità.

In short

Mitayo has no accounts and no servers of its own. You log in with your MyAnimeList account on MyAnimeList's own page, and everything the app uses (your list and the skipped anime) stays on the phone. The app talks to MyAnimeList, to GitHub for updates and to Google Firebase for the news notifications. Usage statistics and crash reports start off and are turned on only if you agree; they never contain your list, the titles or your MyAnimeList name. No ads.

Chi gestisce Mitayo

Mitayo è un progetto personale, gratuito e non commerciale di Emanuele Melini. Non è affiliata a MyAnimeList: usa l'API ufficiale di MyAnimeList secondo il loro accordo per gli sviluppatori.

Who runs Mitayo

Mitayo is a personal, free and non-commercial project by Emanuele Melini. It isn't affiliated with MyAnimeList: it uses the official MyAnimeList API under their developer agreement.

Accesso a MyAnimeList

Il login avviene sulla pagina di MyAnimeList, aperta nel browser (OAuth 2.0). Mitayo non vede né salva la tua password: riceve solo un token di accesso, che conserva sul telefono cifrato con una chiave dell'Android Keystore e rinnova da solo finché resti collegato.

MyAnimeList access

You log in on MyAnimeList's page, opened in the browser (OAuth 2.0). Mitayo neither sees nor stores your password: it only gets an access token, which it keeps on the phone encrypted with an Android Keystore key and renews by itself while you stay logged in.

Quali dati usa

  • Dal tuo account MyAnimeList: nome utente, immagine del profilo e la tua lista anime (stato ed episodi visti di ogni anime).
  • Dal catalogo pubblico di MyAnimeList: titoli, immagini, generi, voti, episodi, stagioni e orario di uscita settimanale degli anime, gli anime in arrivo, e i suggerimenti che MyAnimeList calcola per te.
  • Creati da te nell'app: gli anime ignorati, gli ultimi 100 swipe (per annullarli da Profilo → Dati → Ultimi swipe), le modifiche non ancora inviate e le impostazioni (tema, lingua di titolo e sottotitolo, contenuti per adulti, mazzi mescolati, guida già vista, accettazione di termini e informativa, scelte su statistiche, report dei crash, novità e promemoria, giorno dell'ultimo controllo degli aggiornamenti, ultima versione e ultima stagione annunciate e versione di cui hai già visto le novità).
  • File degli ignorati: se li esporti (Profilo → Dati), un file JSON con gli ID degli anime ignorati, salvato dove scegli tu; Mitayo non lo manda a nessuno.
  • Liste di altri utenti: quando confronti due liste o apri il mazzo di un amico, l'app scarica da MyAnimeList la lista pubblica degli utenti di cui scrivi il nome, con generi, studi, fonte e classificazione di ogni anime per l'affinità dei gusti (calcolata sul telefono). Il confronto resta solo in memoria finché la pagina è aperta; per il mazzo restano sul telefono gli ID degli anime della sua lista, cancellati quando apri il mazzo di un altro, quando esci e comunque entro un giorno. Mitayo non salva altro di loro e non lo manda a nessuno. Gli ultimi nomi utente che hai scritto (al massimo 6) restano per comodità: li cancelli dalla stessa pagina o con Esci.

What data it uses

  • From your MyAnimeList account: username, profile picture and your anime list (status and episodes watched for each anime).
  • From MyAnimeList's public catalog: titles, pictures, genres, scores, episodes, seasons and weekly airing time of the anime, the upcoming anime, and the suggestions MyAnimeList makes for you.
  • Created by you in the app: the skipped anime, the last 100 swipes (to undo them from Profile → Data → Last swipes), the changes not sent yet and the settings (theme, title and subtitle language, adult content, shuffled decks, guide already seen, acceptance of the terms and of this policy, choices about statistics, crash reports, news and reminders, day of the last update check, last version and season announced and version whose news you've already seen).
  • Skipped anime file: if you export them (Profile → Data), a JSON file with the ids of the skipped anime, saved where you choose; Mitayo doesn't send it anywhere.
  • Other users' lists: when you compare two lists or open a friend's deck, the app downloads from MyAnimeList the public list of the users whose name you type, with the genres, studios, source and age rating of each anime for the taste affinity (worked out on the phone). The comparison stays only in memory while the page is open; for the deck, the ids of the anime of their list stay on the phone and are deleted when you open someone else's deck, when you log out and anyway within a day. Mitayo keeps nothing else about them and sends it nowhere. The last user names you typed (6 at most) are kept for convenience: delete them from the same page or with Log out.

Dove restano

Solo sul telefono, nella memoria riservata all'app. Non c'è un server di Mitayo e il backup di Android è disattivato, quindi questi dati non finiscono nel cloud né su un altro telefono. Le immagini degli anime restano in una cache sul telefono per non riscaricarle.

Where they stay

On the phone only, in the app's private storage. There's no Mitayo server and Android backup is off, so these data don't end up in the cloud or on another phone. Anime pictures stay in a cache on the phone so they aren't downloaded again.

Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited), solo nelle versioni pubblicate da GitHub:

Google riceve un codice di registrazione casuale e gli argomenti, nient'altro. Spegnendole in Profilo → Notifiche il codice viene cancellato.

Tutto è legato a un codice casuale dell'installazione, mai al tuo account. Mitayo non usa pubblicità né tracciamento pubblicitario, e non vende i tuoi dati.

Questo sito non usa cookie. La pagina di download legge l'elenco delle versioni dalle API di GitHub direttamente dal tuo browser, quindi GitHub riceve l'indirizzo IP di chi la apre.

Who it talks to

MyAnimeList: myanimelist.net for the login, api.myanimelist.net to read the catalog and your list and to change it (status, episodes watched and, if you give one, the score), for the search when you tap "Search on MyAnimeList", for the public lists of the users you compare or whose deck you open, for your whole list when you open Your statistics (the numbers stay only in memory), cdn.myanimelist.net for the pictures. YouTube: in the details of an anime with trailers, the video previews come from i.ytimg.com, and a video you tap opens in the YouTube app or in the browser, under Google's terms. Your list changes only when you decide so: a right swipe, a swipe up with the episodes watched, +1 in the Watching deck, a score in the To score deck, "Start it" in What to watch?, a status you pick or undoing a swipe. What MyAnimeList does with your data is governed by MyAnimeList's privacy policy.

Share: an anime's MyAnimeList link or a comparison result go to the app you pick from Android's menu; Mitayo sends nothing by itself.

GitHub, for updates: at most once a day the app asks api.github.com for the latest version of Mitayo, sending only the installed version (in the User-Agent header) and, like any request, your IP address. If you tap Update, the APK is downloaded from github.com. Nothing about your MyAnimeList account or your list reaches GitHub. What GitHub does with these data is governed by the GitHub privacy statement.

Reminders: they're notifications made on the phone, all off until you turn them on in Profile → Notifications. "New version" asks GitHub for the latest version once a day; "Season start" downloads the new season's list from MyAnimeList; "Weekly reminder" counts the anime you have left, without internet; "New episodes" checks, without internet, which anime you're watching aired an episode in the last day, from the weekly time MyAnimeList gives for them.

Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited), only in the versions published on GitHub:

  • News (Firebase Cloud Messaging): if you leave them on, after the question about statistics the phone registers to receive notifications and follows the topic news and the one of its language (news-it or news-en). Google gets a random registration code and the topics, nothing else. Turning them off in Profile → Notifications deletes the code.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), only if you agree: the screens opened, the swipes by kind of list (most popular, season, genre without saying which…) and their outcome (completed, watching, skipped, passed to the next, one more episode or another status), scores given (without the score), undos (from the last swipes too), actions on the skipped anime, statuses set from the search and from the related anime and from a comparison, comparisons made, searches made on MyAnimeList (never the text searched or the user names), decks shuffled or not, filters used (in which kind of deck, not which ones), What to watch? (another anime proposed or one started), shares (of an anime or of a comparison, never which), the general settings (theme, title and subtitle language, number of reminders on, news yes or no, app language) and the technical data Firebase collects by itself (phone model, Android and Mitayo versions, approximate area derived from the IP address).
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), only if you agree: where in the code the app stopped, phone model, versions and memory state.

Everything is tied to a random code of the install, never to your account. Your MyAnimeList name, your list, the titles, anime or genre ids and the access token are never sent. The advertising id isn't collected and the data aren't used for ads. Google processes these data on Mitayo's behalf and may transfer them outside the European Union with the safeguards of the GDPR: see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Mitayo uses no ads or ad tracking, and doesn't sell your data.

This site uses no cookies. The download page reads the list of versions from the GitHub API straight from your browser, so GitHub gets the IP address of whoever opens it.

Per quanto tempo

  • Finché resti collegato. Con Esci (Profilo) si cancellano dal telefono il token, la lista, gli ignorati, gli ultimi swipe e le modifiche non ancora inviate; resta solo il catalogo pubblico degli anime.
  • Disinstallando l'app si cancella tutto quello che è sul telefono.
  • Statistiche d'uso: i dati legati all'installazione restano su Firebase al massimo 2 mesi dall'ultimo utilizzo, poi solo in forma aggregata. Report dei crash: 90 giorni.
  • Il codice per le novità scade dopo un lungo periodo senza usare l'app, o subito se le spegni.
  • Quello che MyAnimeList rimuove o modifica viene tolto o aggiornato anche sul telefono entro un giorno: l'app ricontrolla il catalogo una volta al giorno quando la apri, e la tua lista a ogni avvio.

How long

  • As long as you stay logged in. Log out (Profile) deletes from the phone the token, the list, the skipped anime, the last swipes and the changes not sent yet; only the public anime catalog stays.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything that is on the phone.
  • Usage statistics: the data tied to the install stay on Firebase at most 2 months after the last use, then only in aggregate form. Crash reports: 90 days.
  • The news code expires after a long time without using the app, or right away if you turn them off.
  • What MyAnimeList removes or changes is removed or updated on the phone too within a day: the app checks the catalog again once a day when you open it, and your list at every start.

Your rights

Mitayo's data are on your phone: you see them in the app and delete them with Log out or by uninstalling the app. For the data stored on MyAnimeList (account and list) contact MyAnimeList. Mitayo needs a MyAnimeList account, so their age requirements apply too. Adult content is hidden until you turn it on.

You can consent to statistics and crash reports on your own from 14 (in Italy; elsewhere from the age set by your country). If you're younger you need a parent's permission: without it, answer "No thanks".

Statistics and crash reports rely on your consent: after the first login the app asks you, and until you answer (or if you say no) they stay off. You can change your mind any time in Profile → Privacy: turning them off stops the sending, resets the random code and deletes the reports not sent yet. The data controller is Emanuele Melini: to access the data collected by Firebase, have them deleted or exercise your other GDPR rights, write to mitayo@emanuelemelini.dev. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated; if it changes in substance, the app asks you to accept it again. About your data write to mitayo@emanuelemelini.dev; for questions, reports or abuse also an issue on GitHub, which is public though.
