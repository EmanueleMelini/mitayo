- Swipe up (or ▶) sets the anime as watching with the episodes you've watched; a right swipe on an anime still airing asks them too.
- ★ button: completed with a score from 1 to 10, only when you want it (swiping right stays instant).
- Search any downloaded anime by title, or on MyAnimeList, and give it a status without swiping.
- Decks by decade, and sequels and prequels in the details of an anime, with their status to set.
- Skipped anime one by one in Profile → Data: search them, restore one or give it a status; export and import them to a file.
- Title and subtitle in romaji, English or Japanese.
- Notifications: Mitayo news and new versions, plus the reminders you turn on (new version, season start, weekly).
- Updates from the app, with what's new after each one.
- Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, only if you allow them: never your list, the titles or your MAL name.
- New icon, the Profile in pages, smoother animations and shortcuts on the icon for search and season.
- Terms of use and an email for your data: the app asks you to accept terms and privacy policy again.
