- Compare lists: you and another MyAnimeList user, or two users, side by side: totals, score distribution, the anime in common with both scores, the ones only one of you has, and your affinity.
- A friend's deck: type a MyAnimeList name and swipe the anime of their list you don't have yet, theirs first.
- From a comparison you can give a status to the anime of the other list.
- Decks show only the anime that aren't in your list yet; your list has its own decks, one per status (watching, completed, on-hold, dropped, plan to watch). There a left swipe only moves on to the next anime: it comes back the next time.
- ⋯ button in the deck: on-hold, dropped or plan to watch, the statuses no swipe gives.
- Trailers and videos in the details of an anime, opened in YouTube; the details also open from the poster in search, skipped anime and comparisons.
- App language: system, Italiano or English (Profile → Appearance).
